PCB Design Engineer – Wireless & Embedded Systems

Noida Experience: 1-3 years No. of openings: 1

Job Description (JD):

We are looking for a motivated PCB Design Engineer with hands-on experience in multilayer PCB design and basic exposure to RF and wireless communication hardware. The ideal candidate should have experience working with microcontroller-based designs, communication modules, RF layout fundamentals, and EMI/EMC-aware PCB design practices. This role is focused primarily on PCB design and hardware development for wireless and embedded communication systems.

Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Design and develop schematics and PCB layouts using KiCad and Altium Designer.
  • Work on STM32-based controller board designs and embedded hardware systems.
  • Design multilayer PCBs (up to 4 layers) with proper grounding, stack-up, thermal management, and routing practices.
  • Support development of communication hardware involving Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GNSS, telemetry, and RF modules.
  • Perform impedance-controlled routing and follow RF PCB layout best practices.
  • Work with RF-related components such as LNAs, filters, antennas, matching circuits, and RF connectors.
  • Implement EMI/EMC design techniques including filtering, shielding, and grounding optimization.
  • Create and maintain custom PCB footprints and component libraries.
  • Generate Gerber files, BOM, fabrication drawings, pick-and-place files, and assembly documentation.
  • Perform DFM checks and coordinate with PCB fabrication vendors.
  • Develop and assemble hardware prototypes for testing and validation.
  • Support PCB bring-up, debugging, testing, and hardware validation activities.
  • Assist in SMD soldering, rework, and prototype modifications whenever required.
  • Assist in UAV integration, wiring, and system-level testing whenever needed.
  • Prepare hardware documentation, reports, and revision records.
  • Coordinate with hardware, firmware, and mechanical teams during product development.

Desired Skills:

  • 1–3 years of experience in PCB design and hardware development.
  • Hands-on experience with KiCad and Altium Designer.
  • Experience designing STM32-based controller boards and embedded hardware.
  • Strong understanding of PCB design fundamentals and multilayer PCB layout practices.
  • Basic understanding of RF and wireless hardware design concepts.
  • Knowledge of impedance matching, RF grounding, shielding, and signal integrity basics.
  • Familiarity with communication modules such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, GNSS, or telemetry systems.
  • Basic knowledge of EMI/EMC concepts and PCB noise reduction techniques.
  • Hands-on experience in SMD soldering and rework.
  • Experience working with packages such as UDFN, MSOP, QFN, and other fine-pitch SMD components.
  • Familiarity with debugging tools such as Oscilloscope, Logic Analyzer, and basic RF testing equipment.
  • Experience in footprint creation, Gerber generation, and DFM processes.
  • Good documentation and communication skills.
  • Basic experience with RF front-end components including LNAs, filters, antennas, or matching networks (preferred).
  • Exposure to UAV systems, embedded wireless products, or IoT-based products (preferred).
  • Familiarity with IPC standards and RF PCB layout guidelines (preferred).
  • Experience in PCB bring-up and prototype testing (preferred).
  • Basic understanding of interfaces such as UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, USB, or Ethernet (preferred).


Qualification :    B.E / B.Tech in Electronics, ECE, EEE, Instrumentation, or related field.

PCB Design Engineer – Power Electronics

Noida Experience: 1-3 years No. of openings: 1

Job Description (JD):

We are looking for a skilled and detail-oriented PCB Design Engineer to join our Hardware Design/Power Electronics team. The ideal candidate should have hands-on experience in multilayer PCB design (up to 4 layers), power electronics, and high-voltage systems. The role involves schematic design, PCB layout optimization, EMI/EMC-aware design practices, hardware validation, and support in UAV hardware integration activities.

Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Design and develop schematics and PCB layouts using KiCad and Altium Designer.
  • Work on power supply designs including DC-DC converters, AC-DC converters, battery-powered systems, BMS, and high-voltage circuits.
  • Design multilayer PCBs (up to 4 layers) considering grounding, thermal performance, manufacturability, and signal integrity.
  • Create and maintain custom PCB footprints and component libraries.
  • Generate manufacturing deliverables including Gerber files, BOM, fabrication drawings, assembly files, and production documentation.
  • Perform DFM (Design for Manufacturability) checks and coordinate with PCB fabrication and assembly vendors.
  • Implement EMI/EMC design techniques including filtering, shielding, grounding strategies, and noise reduction methods.
  • Perform impedance-controlled routing and follow best practices for high-speed and high-current PCB layouts.
  • Support PCB bring-up, debugging, testing, and validation activities.
  • Assist in UAV hardware integration, wiring, and system-level testing activities.
  • Prepare hardware documentation, design reports, testing reports, and revision records.
  • Coordinate with hardware, firmware, and mechanical teams during product development.

Desired Skills:

  • Hands-on experience with KiCad and Altium Designer.
  • Strong understanding of PCB design fundamentals and multilayer board stack-up.
  • Experience in power electronics and power supply hardware design.
  • Good knowledge of EMI/EMC concepts and mitigation techniques.
  • Understanding of impedance matching, grounding strategies, and signal integrity.
  • Knowledge of high-voltage safety practices, isolation techniques, and thermal management.
  • Hands-on experience in SMD soldering and rework of packages like uDFN, QFN, etc.
  • Familiarity with hardware debugging tools such as Oscilloscope, Multimeter, Logic Analyzer, and Power Analyzer.
  • Good understanding of PCB manufacturing, assembly, and DFM processes.
  • Experience in footprint creation and component library management.
  • Good documentation and communication skills.
  • Experience with industrial power systems, UAV electronics, EV electronics, or battery systems (preferred).
  • Familiarity with IPC standards and PCB layout guidelines (preferred).
  • Exposure to high-current and thermally demanding applications (preferred).
  • Experience in PCB bring-up and prototype validation (preferred).
  • Basic understanding of embedded hardware systems and interfaces (preferred).


Qualification :    B.E / B.Tech / M.E / M.Tech in Electronics, ECE, EEE, Instrumentation, or related field.

Podili and Noida Experience: 4 to 8 years No. of openings: 1

Job Description (JD):

We are seeking an experienced Senior Aerostructures Design Engineer to develop, integrate and implement technical solutions for our mission critical programmes of work. The role involves shared responsibility in the design of structural aircraft and components, identifying technical problems and creating technical solutions across all phases of a programme from conceptual design to product maturation.

Essential skills:

  • Must have the ability to act independently and build a team of associates skilled on aero structure production.
  • Knowledge aero structures - Wings, Fuselage, Empennage
  • The Design Engineer will develop, integrate and implement technical solutions for some of our mission critical programmes of work.
  • Ensure designs are compliant with all relevant customer, regulatory, and airworthiness requirements and internal standards within the programme, schedule, and manufacturing constraints.
  • Have a shared responsibility in the design of structural aircraft and components, identifying technical problems and creating technical solutions.
  • Work with both metal and composite structures and different assembly techniques.
  • Use a variety of methods and tools, for example CAD (experience using CATIA V5 and preferably NX / 3DX) software.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Oversee a small team of structure engineers to guide, direct, design and production, based on the order quantities within stipulated timelines.
  • Wing, Empennage Fuselage Aero structures detailed design & development and support production requirements where necessary.
  • The design engineer will be part of a multidisciplinary team together with AI engineers, manufacturing engineers, R&D and flight operations teams.
  • Working in all phases of a programme, starting with the conceptual phase, the full-scale phase in which the detailed design is translated into product drawings/ models, up to the product maturation phase assisting with problems during manufacturing and proposing changes to the detailed design in order to contribute to weight and cost savings.
  • Work on the integration of all requirements and input from other engineering disciplines into the conceptual and detailed design, such that the product definition can be made and a cost-efficient and producible design is achieved.
  • Propose design solutions, based on the correct balance of cost, quality, manufacturability, serviceability and performance.
  • To communicate effectively with co-worker, customer and suppliers
  • Design or amendment of Components, Products to a specification.
  • Update the Bill of Materials (BOM) and write the Engineering Change Note (ECN)

Preferable prerequisites:

  • Prior experience in the below mentioned:

    • Design reports

Aeronautical Engineer – Fixed Wing UAV Development

Podili, Ongole, Andhra Pradesh A-38, sector 67, Noida, Uttar Pradesh - 201301 Department: Research & Development Employment Type: Full Time

Job Description (JD):

We are seeking an experienced and highly motivated Aeronautical Engineer to lead and execute the design and development of a fixed-wing Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV). The candidate must possess strong technical expertise in aerodynamics, aircraft structures, and overall aircraft design, and be capable of independently managing the complete project lifecycle—from conceptual design to flight testing and validation.

This role requires a hands-on engineer with leadership ability, analytical thinking, and practical experience in UAV or aircraft development programs.

Roles and Responsibilities:

  • Lead the complete design and development of a large fixed-wing UAV.
  • Perform aerodynamic design, analysis, and optimization of aircraft configurations.
  • Conduct structural design and analysis including material selection, load calculations, and stress analysis.
  • Develop conceptual, preliminary, and detailed aircraft design documentation.
  • Prepare weight estimation, performance estimation, stability, and control analysis.
  • Coordinate with avionics, propulsion, and manufacturing teams.
  • Create detailed 3D CAD models, engineering drawings, and manufacturing documentation.
  • Oversee prototype fabrication, assembly, and system integration.
  • Plan and supervise ground tests, structural tests, and flight trials.
  • Ensure compliance with applicable aviation standards and safety requirements.
  • Manage project timelines, technical reviews, and milestone tracking.
  • Interact with vendors and suppliers for materials and components.
  • Troubleshoot design, integration, and performance issues during development and testing.

Desired Skills:

  • Strong understanding of aircraft aerodynamics.
  • In-depth knowledge of flight mechanics.
  • Solid expertise in aircraft structures.
  • Experience with stability and control analysis.
  • Knowledge of propulsion system integration.
  • Hands-on experience in fixed-wing aircraft or UAV design and development.
  • Experience with composite structures and manufacturing processes.
  • Capability in structural load analysis and finite element analysis (FEA).
  • Proficiency in CATIA, SolidWorks, or NX.
  • Experience using ANSYS, NASTRAN, or Abaqus.
  • Working knowledge of XFLR5, MATLAB, or similar analysis tools.
  • Ability to generate detailed design reports, test plans, performance estimation sheets, and technical documentation.
  • Strong analytical, problem-solving, and project management skills.
  • Ability to work independently and lead technical teams.
  • Excellent communication and documentation skills.
  • Willingness to participate in field trials and travel as required.


Qualification :    B.E / B.Tech / M.Tech in Aeronautical or Aerospace Engineering.

Experience :    3 – 7 Years (UAV / Aircraft Design).

Department :    Research & Development
