DEFENCE & SPACE RESEARCH INSTITUTE

FOR THE NATION, BEYOND THE HORIZON

ABOUT DSRI

The Defence & Space Research Institute (DSRI) is a premier research organization dedicated to advancing India's defence and space capabilities. Our mission is to conduct cutting-edge research, develop innovative technologies, and provide strategic solutions to meet the nation's defence and space needs.

OBJECTIVES

  • Foster Indigenous Innovation
    Advance self-reliant research and development to empower India's defence and space capabilities.
  • Integrate Science, Strategy, and Collaboration
    Unite academia, industry, and defence to transform visionary ideas into mission-ready technologies.
  • Inspire Excellence and Exploration
    Cultivate a new generation of innovators to push the boundaries of knowledge, security, and space exploration.
  • Collaborations
    Encouraging IIT and other Engineering Institutes for co-developing Defence technologies

FUTURE

The Defence & Space Research Institute (DSRI) will play a significant role in advancing India's defence and space capabilities. With the government's focus on innovation and self-reliance, DSRI is expected to receive continued support and funding.

Some potential areas of focus for DSRI's future include: Advanced Defence Technologies — developing cutting-edge defence systems, such as  hypersonic missiles and stealth technology,  Space Exploration — contributing to India's space program,  including missions to the Moon,  Mars,  and beyond,  Artificial Intelligence — applying AI to defence and space applications,  such as autonomous systems and predictive maintenance,  Cybersecurity — enhancing India's cybersecurity capabilities to protect critical infrastructure and defence systems.
