JOHNNETTE JB-1 / MODULAR ARCHITECTURE

MISSION-READY.
CONFIGURABLE.

The USV supports a modular payload architecture, enabling integration of mission-specific payload systems for surveillance, reconnaissance, logistics, strike and attack operations. Payloads can be configured according to operational requirements, providing enhanced situational awareness, autonomous mission execution and flexible mission support.

EO/IR Camera

Day/night imaging, surveillance and target observation.

LRF – Laser Range Finder

Long-range distance measurement and enhanced situational awareness.

Logistics Cargo Module

Configurable compartment for transportation of mission equipment and essential supplies.

Warhead / Mission Effector

Modular interface for integration of authorised mission-specific payloads.
