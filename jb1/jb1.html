Johnnette JB1 is an Unmanned Surface Vessel (USV) designed for remotely controlled as well as semi-autonomous surveillance missions, enabling users to deploy it in chosen designated areas. This state-of-the-art vessel is designed for precise way- point navigation in autonomous mode. The compact design of the vessel makes it almost imperceptible, rendering it an excellent choice for stealth operations both during the day and at night. The JB1 is adept at conducting Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR) activities.

The unmanned platform provides an avenue for reduction of risk to human life, reduction in demand on capital assets and significantly enhance surveillance capability in terms of range and duration.

JOHNNETTE JB-1 / MODULAR SYSTEMS

PAYLOADS

The USV supports a modular payload architecture, enabling integration of mission-specific payload systems for surveillance, reconnaissance, logistics, strike and attack operations. Payloads can be configured according to operational requirements, providing enhanced situational awareness, autonomous mission execution and flexible mission support.

01 / IMAGING

EO/IR Camera

Day-and-night imaging for surveillance, observation and situational awareness.

02 / RANGING

Laser Range Finder

Long-range distance measurement to support observation and situational awareness.

03 / LOGISTICS

Logistics Cargo Module

Configurable storage for transporting mission equipment and essential supplies.

04 / MISSION SYSTEMS

Mission Effector

Payload configuration for authorised, mission-specific integrations.
