  Course Overview

A Drone Pilot Certificate/ Remote Pilot Certificate (RPC) is issued by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) approved Remote Pilot Training Organisation (RPTO) after fulfilling the conditions for issue under The Drone Rules, 2021. It is an integrated approach to training where the application of knowledge happens along-side your practical training, improving skill development. Getting a certificate qualification in the drone industry has become a requirement to fly a UAV (Drone) now, and we ensure you develop the Skills and Attitude to be a proficient drone pilot and to display confidence, bringing success in your employment pursuit.

    Eligibility Criteria

    • 18 to 65 years of age at the time of enrollment.
    • Should have cleared class 10th or its equivalent from a recognised board.
    • Class 3 medical certificate signed by any medical practitioner (MBBS).
    • Government issued proof of identity and government issued proof of address(Voter's ID card, Ration Card or Driving License).

    About the RPC Course

    The RPC course is designed to provide individuals with the necessary knowledge and skills to operate drones safely and in compliance with DGCA regulations.

    The course may cover topics such as:

    • Aerodynamics and flight principles of drones.
    • DGCA regulations and airspace restrictions.
    • Weather conditions and their impact on drone operations.
    • Drone technology and maintenance.
    • Flight planning and risk assessment.
    • Emergency procedures and safety protocols.
    • Navigation and communication systems.
    • Practical flight training (if applicable).
