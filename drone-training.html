Eligibility Criteria

  • 18 to 65 years of age at the time of enrollment.
  • Shall have passed Any Degree from a recognised University
  • Class 3 medical certificate signed by any medical practitioner (MBBS).
  • Government issued proof of identity and government issued proof of address(Voter's ID card, Ration Card or Driving License).
  • Aadhaar card copy.
  • 10th marksheet.

Key Offerings and Duration of Course: 5 Days

The course is covered in 05 days as per the following schedule:

  • Theory Sessions – 16 hrs.
  • Simulator Sessions - 3 hrs. (Minimum),
  • Practical Lab Session - 2.5 hrs.
  • Practical Flying Sessions – 4.5 hrs.
